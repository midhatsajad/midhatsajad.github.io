Education

My academic journey has been shaped by curiosity, persistence, and a desire to understand the challenges involved in underground engineering. Through my studies and research, I've developed a strong foundation in rock mechanics, tunneling, and TBM engineering while continuing to explore new approaches to engineering problems. Each stage of my education has contributed to the researcher and engineer I am becoming, while also encouraging me to keep learning beyond the classroom.

  • PhD, Underground Construction and Tunnel Engineering, Colorado School of Mines
  • Graduate Certificate, Underground Construction and Tunnel Engineering, Colorado School of Mines
  • M.Tech, Tunnelling and Underground Space Technology, Indian Institute of Technology (ISM), Dhanbad (Gold Medalist)
  • B.E., Civil Engineering, University of Kashmir

Learning & Growing

None of that learning stopped at a degree. Just as much of it has come from research problems that didn't go the way I expected, from colleagues who saw a problem differently than I did, from mistakes I had to go back and fix, and from picking up technical skills I never took a class in. Some of the most useful lessons came from experiences that had nothing to do with engineering at all. School gave me a foundation; everything since has been building on it.
