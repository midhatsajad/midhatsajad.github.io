Experience

Every experience has been an opportunity to learn something new. My academic and professional journey has allowed me to work across different aspects of tunneling and underground construction, from research and data analysis to engineering applications and industry projects. Along the way, I've had the opportunity to collaborate with people from different backgrounds, work with real-world engineering problems, and develop a deeper understanding of how research and practice can complement each other. This section highlights some of the experiences and projects that have shaped my professional journey.

Tunneling and Underground Space Intern

ARUP, San Francisco, CA

Worked on the BART CP2 project in California and the Bloor–Yonge project in Toronto.

Geotech and Tunneling Intern

WSP, Lakewood, CO

Developed geotechnical baseline reports and schedules for major tunneling projects including the MWRA Boston tunnel and I-94, and contributed to precast segmental lining design. Supported micro-tunneling projects in the US and Canada with jacking-force calculations for vertical curve alignments.

Teaching Assistant

Colorado School of Mines, Golden, CO

Teaching Rock Mechanics and Tunnelling, and working with faculty at the Earth Mechanics Institute.

Assistant Engineering Manager

Larsen & Toubro, EDRC – Metro and Railways, India

Designed and managed major metro and railway projects: rock-support interaction analysis, ground improvement, slope stability, and tunnel structure design. Led international work including the Mauritius Metro Light Rail and Riyadh Metro Line 3, and contributed to the Delhi–Meerut RRTS, Mumbai–Ahmedabad High Speed Rail, and Chennai Metro Rail, among others.

Teaching Assistant

Indian Institute of Technology (ISM), Dhanbad, India

Taught alongside faculty: lectures, course materials, grading, and lab mentoring for undergraduates.

Engineering Intern

Al-Farouk for Engineering Works Co., Cairo, Egypt

Planned and scheduled an underground optical fiber network for Nasr City, Cairo, including cost estimation and site inspection.
