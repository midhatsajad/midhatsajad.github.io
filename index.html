Midhat Sajad

Tunnel design engineer turned PhD researcher, building simulation tools for underground construction.

Now

I'm a PhD student in Underground Construction and Tunnel Engineering at Colorado School of Mines, working with Prof. Jamal Rostami and Prof. Priscilla Nelson at the Earth Mechanics Institute. My research is a discrete event simulation model for predicting TBM (tunnel boring machine) utilization and performance, one part of a larger cloud-based simulation app I'm building for the work.

Alongside the PhD, I teach MNGN321 (Introduction to Rock Mechanics) and MNGN404 (Tunnelling) as a teaching assistant, and I'm interning with ARUP's Tunneling and Underground Space group, currently on the BART CP2 project in California and the Bloor-Yonge project in Toronto.
