Tunnel design engineer turned PhD researcher, building simulation tools for underground construction.

Introduction

I'm a PhD student in Underground Construction and Tunnel Engineering at Colorado School of Mines, working with Prof. Jamal Rostami. My research is to develop a discrete event simulation model for predicting TBM (tunnel boring machine) utilization and performance, one part of a larger cloud-based simulation app I'm building for the work.

Alongside the PhD, I've also taught MNGN321 (Introduction to Rock Mechanics) and MNGN404 (Tunnelling) as a teaching assistant, and I've previously interned with WSP and ARUP's Tunneling and Underground Space group.
