Travelling

I love travelling and discovering new places, cultures, landscapes, and experiences. It's less about checking a place off a list and more about the small moments that turn into memories: a conversation with a stranger, a meal I can't stop thinking about, a view worth the climb, and the chance to try things I'd never come across at home. Every journey adds a new perspective, and some places stay with you long after you leave.
