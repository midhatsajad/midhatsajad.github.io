Hobbies

Beyond the Research

There is a lot more to me than research, engineering, and academic work. When I step away from my computer and research problems, I enjoy exploring new places, trying new food, traveling, spending time with family and friends, and simply enjoying the everyday moments that often become the best memories. This page is about those parts of my life: the interests, experiences, and little things that make me who I am outside of my work.

I enjoy hiking, gardening, traveling and exploring the outdoors. I also like to cook in my free time and try different recipes, and I still can't quite leave tunnels behind even off the clock.
